Oathfire · The Hollow March

An original mobile browser adventure built for Chris Dayley. World design, story, equipment, interface, progression, combat, custom animation layers and procedural sound design created for this game.

Animated character foundations

KayKit Adventurers by Kay Lousberg. CC0 1.0. Source commit 672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47. License.

KayKit Skeletons by Kay Lousberg. CC0 1.0. Source commit 15b62b9bad122f72926c10fb14d622c73819fa54. License.

Version 1.1 uses the Knight animation rig and shared skeletal clips from the Adventurers pack. The original visible body meshes are removed at runtime. Original Oathfire armor, anatomy and equipment are bound to the retargeted skeleton; movement, casting and cloth receive game-specific animation layers. Retired source models remain in the repository but are excluded from the offline game cache. Mounts and environment geometry are created for Oathfire.

The Royal Orchestra

Original Oathfire melodies, arrangements and adaptive composition. The orchestra uses 82 recorded samples from VSCO 2 Community Edition, by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell, with sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. The instrument recordings are CC0; they are not recordings of an orchestra performing these pieces together.

Strings, brass, woodwinds, harp, timpani, bells and percussion are mixed locally with a concert-hall impulse. Source commit 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e; SFZ pitch mapping 6dd651d55dde97fd4028699be9d4481f26917891. Sample provenance and hashes · CC0 license · Listen to the soundtrack.

Art direction

The design studies are aspirational painted concept art. The playable game uses original real-time character geometry, scanned materials, warm stone, teal-and-gold heraldry and wide landscapes. Version 1.1 is a refined art pass with eight distinct enemy designs; it does not reproduce the detail of the paintings. Concept paintings are not gameplay screenshots.

Your progress

Oathfire uses local browser storage and IndexedDB for saves. Export or import your campaign in Settings. No account, analytics, advertisements or real-money purchases are used.
