Oathfire · The Hollow March

An original mobile browser adventure built for Chris Dayley. World design, story, equipment, interface, progression, combat, custom animation layers and procedural sound design created for this game.

Animated character foundations

KayKit Adventurers by Kay Lousberg. CC0 1.0. Source commit 672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47. License.

KayKit Skeletons by Kay Lousberg. CC0 1.0. Source commit 15b62b9bad122f72926c10fb14d622c73819fa54. License.

Version 1.1 uses the Knight animation rig and shared skeletal clips from the Adventurers pack. The original visible body meshes are removed at runtime. Original Oathfire armor, anatomy and equipment are bound to the retargeted skeleton; movement, casting and cloth receive game-specific animation layers. Retired source models remain in the repository but are excluded from the offline game cache. Mounts and environment geometry are created for Oathfire.

Anatomical characters

Adult head geometry, the Ashwright’s muscular arms, and the skin texture are derived from MakeHuman system assets, CC0 1.0. The head and weighted arm surfaces are retargeted onto the existing game skeleton. Arm mesh sources, hashes and build recipe. See the license overview, full CC0 license and source URLs and hashes. Garments, heraldry, weapons and rank arrangements are built for Oathfire; reused armor pieces are credited below.

Tailored heroes — 2.24

The current hero bodies use original fitted armor and garment meshes over the existing animation rig, with class-specific face sculpting. Dedicated cloth, leather and metal atlas textures and age/character treatments of the MakeHuman skin atlas were generated with OpenAI image generation. Underlying anatomical assets retain their CC0 attribution above.

Royal Atelier — 2.3

All three heroes and the Longbowman use editable Blender meshes. The Warden’s fitted breastplate and helmet derive from Knight (Rigged - Mid Poly) by crownjoshua, CC0. Attribution. Other garments and fittings are built for Oathfire.

Castle doors by Tina, wooden barrels and lanterns by James Ray Cock, and round wooden tables by Ulan Cabanilla, via Poly Haven, CC0. Sources and game-file hashes. Original brocade and leaf texture bitmaps were generated with OpenAI image generation.

Recorded orchestral music

Music by Scott Buckley — released under CC BY 4.0. Tracks: Three Sheets to the Wind, Into the Wilds, Call to Adventure, Legionnaire, Monomyth — The Fury, Terminus, Vanguard and Eyes in the Void. These finished stereo productions replace the earlier note sequencer. They are not claimed to be newly recorded live-orchestra performances.

The recordings are encoded to 160 kbps AAC stereo; gameplay selects and crossfades cues as waves progress. Track attributions · Sources and file hashes. No Final Fantasy or Chrono recordings or melodies are included. The retired sample banks retain their original licenses.

Original illustrated campaign atlas created for Oathfire with OpenAI image generation.

Footsteps, armor & elemental sound

Original layered weapon and spell effects, using CC0 recordings from Kenney Impact Sounds and RPG Audio; HaelDB armor footsteps; TinyWorlds surface footsteps; and Peludo water splashes (creator’s games).

Recorded blade-on-blade clashes by StarNinjas (CC0) form the quick and charged sword impacts. Arrow-on-armor uses layered plate, shaft and body recordings. Combat recordings, source hashes and cues.

Recordings are trimmed, filtered, pitch-varied and layered with original elemental textures. Multiple recorded variations cover movement, weapon swings, contact materials, spells and upgrades. Source downloads and verified hashes · Credits and CC0 license · Kenney Impact notice · Kenney RPG notice. No Skyrim audio is included.

Art direction

The design studies are aspirational painted concept art. The playable game uses original real-time character geometry, scanned materials, warm stone, teal-and-gold heraldry and wide landscapes. Version 2.1 adds anatomical faces, ten-stage regiment dress, enemy sallets and furnace plate, embroidered cloth, a rebuilt castle skyline, and photographed sunset illumination. These are real-time models, and still do not reproduce all of the detail in the paintings. Concept paintings are not gameplay screenshots.
